About Verdant Valley

Verdant Valley Landscaping LLC was founded in 2011 by Marco Delgado, who started out mowing lawns for neighbors around Maple Hollow with a single truck and trailer. Fifteen years later, we're a licensed, insured crew serving homeowners across Green Valley County — but we still show up to every estimate ourselves, and our own crews still do the work.
