Common questions

How much does a project typically cost?

It depends on scope, but most patio projects run $6,000–$18,000 and routine lawn care starts around $65 per visit. Every estimate is free and no-obligation — see our Services page for typical ranges.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Verdant Valley carries full liability insurance and holds State Home Improvement Contractor license #0654321. Details are on our About page.

What towns do you serve?

Maple Hollow, Cedar Ridge, Willow Brook, Pine Harbor, Oak Meadow, and Birch Falls, ST. If you're just outside that list, ask — we sometimes take nearby jobs.

How fast can you get to my project?

Estimates are usually scheduled within 3–5 business days. Larger installs (patios, walls) typically start 2–6 weeks out depending on season; routine maintenance can often start the same week.

Do I need to be home for the estimate?

Not always — for lawn care and cleanups we can often quote from photos and a site visit. For patios, walls, and planting design we'll want to walk the property with you.
