Shingle Roof Replacement

A full tear-off down to the deck, new ice-and-water shield along the eaves and valleys, synthetic underlayment, new drip edge and flashing, and architectural shingles from a major manufacturer. We protect your landscaping, magnet-sweep the yard for nails, and back the job with a 25-year workmanship warranty.

Typical range: $9,500–$22,000 for most single-family homes, depending on roof size, pitch, and layers to remove.

Get a Roof Replacement Estimate

Metal Roofing

Standing-seam and stamped metal shingle roofs that last 50+ years, shed snow, and resist wind. A good fit for farmhouses, porches, and anyone who never wants to re-roof again.

Typical range: $18,000–$38,000 for a full home, or $4,000–$9,000 for porches and accent roofs.

Get a Metal Roof Estimate

Flat & Commercial Roofing

TPO and EPDM membrane systems for low-slope roofs, additions, garages, and small commercial buildings, with properly tapered insulation so water drains instead of ponding.

Typical range: $8–$14 per square foot installed, depending on insulation and access.

Get a Flat Roof Estimate

Roof Repair & Leaks

Missing or lifted shingles, leaking chimney and skylight flashing, failed pipe boots, and soft decking. We find the actual source of the leak, show you photos, and fix it right, often the same week you call.

Typical range: $350–$1,500 for most repairs. Diagnosis is free with any repair.

Request a Repair

Storm Damage & Insurance Claims

After wind or hail, we inspect for free and give you a photo report. If there is damage, we meet your adjuster on the roof and handle the paperwork for repairs or replacement. If water is coming in, we tarp the roof the same day whenever possible.

If your claim is approved, you typically pay only your deductible. Emergency tarping starts at $350.

Request a Storm Inspection

Inspections & Maintenance

A 21-point inspection covering shingles, flashing, vents, gutters, attic ventilation, and signs of moisture, with a photo report you keep. Our yearly maintenance plan catches small problems before they turn into leaks.

Inspections are free. Maintenance plans start at $199 per year.
