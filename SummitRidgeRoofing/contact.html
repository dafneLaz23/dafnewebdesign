Common questions

How much does a new roof cost?

Most single-family homes in our area fall between $9,500 and $22,000 for an architectural shingle roof, depending on size, pitch, and how many layers we remove. Your written estimate is free, and the price we quote is the price you pay. See our Services page for typical ranges.

Will my insurance cover it?

If the damage came from a covered event like wind or hail, often yes, minus your deductible. We inspect for free, give you a photo report, and meet your adjuster. If there's no storm damage, we'll tell you that too.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. Monthly payment plans are available on approved credit. We can go over the options during your estimate, and applying won't obligate you to anything.

Do I need a full replacement, or just a repair?

Often just a repair. We send photos of exactly what we find so you can see it yourself. Many of our customers called about a "new roof" and only needed new flashing.

How long does installation take?

Most homes take 1–2 days. We protect your landscaping, keep the driveway clear by the end of each day, and magnet-sweep the yard for nails.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We hold Connecticut Home Improvement Contractor license #0612345 and carry general liability and workers' compensation coverage on every job. Details are on our About page.
