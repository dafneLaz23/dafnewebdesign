About Summit Ridge Roofing

Summit Ridge Roofing was founded in 2009 by Marcus Delgado, a third-generation roofer who started out doing repairs around Maple Hollow from a single pickup truck. Today we're a licensed, insured crew that has completed 1,200+ roofs across Hartwell County. Marcus still walks every estimate himself, and our own crew still does every install.
