Common questions

How much does a new roof cost?

Most full replacements in Hartford County run $12,000–$24,000, depending on the size and pitch of the roof and how many old layers come off. Repairs usually run $350–$1,800. Every estimate is free and written — see our Services page for typical ranges.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. With approved credit you can spread a roof replacement over low monthly payments. Checking your options won’t affect your credit score, and we’ll go over the numbers at your estimate.

My roof is leaking right now. What do I do?

Call (860) 555-0142 any time, day or night. Move valuables away from the drip, put a bucket under it, and we’ll get a tarp on as fast as we can — usually the same day.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most homes take one to two days. We protect your landscaping, clean up every day, and sweep the yard and driveway with a magnet for nails before we leave.

Will you work with my insurance company?

Yes. For storm damage we document everything with photos and can meet your adjuster on site. You only pay your deductible on approved claims.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We hold CT HIC #0612345 and carry general liability and workers’ compensation insurance. Details are on our About page.

What towns do you serve?

Glastonbury, Manchester, South Windsor, Wethersfield, Rocky Hill, and Marlborough, CT. If you’re just outside that list, ask — we often take nearby jobs.
