Roof Replacement

We tear off down to the deck, replace any soft or rotted sheathing, and install a complete shingle system: ice & water shield at the eaves and valleys, synthetic underlayment, new drip edge and flashing, architectural shingles, and a ridge vent. Most homes are done in one to two days, and we cover your landscaping and sweep the yard with a magnet before we leave.

Typical range: $12,000–$24,000 for most Hartford County homes, depending on size, pitch, and layers to remove. With financing, many homeowners pay roughly $150–$300 a month.

Roof Repair & Leaks

Leaks almost always come from flashing, pipe boots, or a few lifted shingles — not the whole roof. We find the actual source, fix it properly, and tell you honestly whether the roof has years left or is due for replacement. Leaking right now? Our emergency line is answered 24/7, and we can tarp the same day.

Typical range: $350–$1,800 for most repairs. Emergency tarping is quoted on the phone before we come out.

Get a Roof Repair Estimate

Storm Damage & Insurance

After wind, hail, or a fallen limb, we secure the roof first, then document every area of damage with dated photos. We’ll meet your insurance adjuster on site so nothing gets missed, and we only start the work once you know what your policy covers.

Typical cost to you: your insurance deductible on approved claims. Inspections after a storm are always free.

Get a Storm Damage Inspection

Gutters & Gutter Guards

Seamless aluminum gutters formed on site to fit your house, with 5- or 6-inch profiles and oversized downspouts for heavy Connecticut rain. Add micro-mesh gutter guards and you’re done climbing ladders every fall.

Typical range: $1,200–$3,500 for most homes, guards included or added separately.

Get a Gutter Estimate

Skylights & Ventilation

Old skylights are one of the most common leak sources we see. We replace them with properly flashed units, and we balance intake and exhaust ventilation so your attic stays dry, your shingles last longer, and ice dams are less likely in January.

Typical range: $1,500–$4,000 per skylight installed; ventilation upgrades from $600.

Free Roof Inspections

Buying, selling, or just not sure how much life your roof has left? Nick inspects every roof personally: shingles, flashing, vents, gutters, and the attic. You get a written photo report and a straight answer — even if the answer is “you don’t need us yet.”

Cost: free for homeowners in our service area. No pressure, no obligation.
