Built for the Connecticut Shoreline

Salt air, nor’easters and freeze-thaw winters are hard on a house. Northpoint Builders designs and builds homes, additions and exteriors that are made for it — with high-wind framing, coastal-grade materials and details that keep water where it belongs.

We work with homeowners in Madison, Guilford, Branford, Clinton, Westbrook, Old Saybrook, Killingworth and Essex. From a classic cape addition to a full custom build, every project gets a fixed-price proposal, one project manager and a crew that cleans up every day.
