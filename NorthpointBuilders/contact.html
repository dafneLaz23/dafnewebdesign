FAQ

Common Questions

How much will my project cost?

It depends on size, site and finishes, but our Services page lists typical ranges for each type of project. After the free on-site visit you’ll get a written, fixed-price proposal.

Do you handle design and permits?

Yes. We produce the drawings, coordinate with engineers when needed, and pull every permit — including coastal and flood-zone approvals.

How long until you can start?

Estimates are usually scheduled within a week. Most projects start 6–12 weeks after you sign, depending on design, permits and the season.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We hold CT HIC #0623456 and CT NHC #0009876, and carry general liability and workers’ compensation insurance. Details are on our About page.

Will I have to move out during the work?

For most additions, roofing and exterior work, no. For whole-home or kitchen renovations we’ll plan the phases with you so you know exactly which weeks are disruptive.

What towns do you serve?

Madison, Guilford, Branford, Clinton, Westbrook, Old Saybrook, Killingworth and Essex, CT. Just outside that list? Ask — we often take nearby projects.
