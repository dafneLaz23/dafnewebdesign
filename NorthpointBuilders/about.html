About Northpoint

A family-owned builder on the Connecticut shoreline since 2006.

Our Story

Shoreline Builders, Start to Finish

Owen Marsh founded Northpoint Builders in 2006 after fifteen years framing homes from Branford to Old Saybrook. He saw too many homeowners juggling an architect, a builder and a dozen subcontractors — and too many projects that ran over budget because nobody owned the whole job.

Northpoint was built to fix that. One team designs it, prices it and builds it, with a fixed-price proposal and a project manager who answers your call. Today we’re a crew of 18, still family-owned, and still building almost entirely within 20 minutes of Madison.

How It Works

Design. Build. Done.

  1. Free Consultation

    We walk the property with you, listen to the plan, and talk honestly about budget and timeline.

  2. Design & Fixed Price

    Drawings, selections and a written, fixed-price proposal — so you know the number before we start.

  3. One Point of Contact

    Your project manager runs the schedule, the crew and the inspectors, and sends a weekly update.

  4. Walkthrough & Warranty

    We finish the punch list together and back the work with a two-year written workmanship warranty.
