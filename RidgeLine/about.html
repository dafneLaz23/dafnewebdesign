About Ridgeline

Ridgeline Lawn & Landscape was founded in 2011 by Jordan Whitfield, who started out mowing lawns for neighbors around Trumbull with a single truck and trailer. Fifteen years later, we're a licensed, insured crew serving homeowners across Fairfield County — but we still show up to every estimate ourselves, and our own crews still do the work.
