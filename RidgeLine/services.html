Lawn Care & Maintenance

Weekly and bi-weekly mowing, edging, and trimming, plus seasonal fertilization and weed control programs. We adjust mowing height by season and sharpen blades regularly so your lawn heals cleanly instead of getting torn.

Typical range: $65–$120 per visit, depending on lot size and frequency.

Patios & Walkways

Paver patios, front walkways, and fire pit surrounds, built on a proper compacted base so they stay level through freeze-thaw cycles instead of heaving after one Connecticut winter.

Typical range: $6,000–$18,000 for a standard patio, depending on size and material.

Get a Patio Estimate

Retaining Walls

Engineered retaining walls for sloped yards and drainage problems, using segmental block or natural fieldstone with proper drainage backfill so the wall doesn't fail after a few wet seasons.

Typical range: $45–$85 per square face foot, depending on material and wall height.

Get a Retaining Wall Estimate

Planting & Landscape Design

Foundation planting, perennial garden beds, and full landscape design plans matched to your soil, sun exposure, and how much upkeep you actually want to do.

Typical range: $1,200–$6,500 depending on bed size and plant selection.

Get a Planting Estimate

Mulch & Bed Maintenance

Fresh mulch installation, bed edging, and ongoing weeding to keep planting beds looking sharp through the growing season.

Typical range: $85–$120 per cubic yard installed.

Get a Mulch Estimate

Spring & Fall Cleanups

Leaf removal, bed clearing, and lawn prep at the start and end of the season, so your property looks its best going into spring planting or winter dormancy.

Typical range: $250–$650 per visit, depending on lot size.
